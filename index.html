Understand the AQI

Know what different air-quality levels mean.

Good

0–50

Little or no health concern.

Satisfactory

51–100

Generally acceptable air quality.

Moderate

101–200

Sensitive people may experience discomfort.

Poor

201–300

Consider limiting long outdoor activity.

Very Poor

301–400

Limit outdoor activity where possible.

Severe

401–500

Avoid outdoor exertion when possible.
